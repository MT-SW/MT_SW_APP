Route recording; pictures from the camera or the gallery (many at once) go to points on the route, and a point can hold a whole stack of pictures. Gallery pictures place themselves by the time and position stored in the picture, and everything can be changed by hand: move a picture to another point, slide a point along the route, add a point name and a picture caption, choose the picture to send (the first by default). On the map the points are drops with a round thumbnail (a tap opens the point), and the same drops show on the position history map of the own node. Replay (the play button in the list) moves a dot along the route and shows slides with pictures and captions at each point. Sharing: the route (with the start and end cuttable) and one picture of each point go to a public picture host, and only a link goes over the mesh; under a received message a "Replay the hike" button saves the hike on the receiver's device and replays it on the map. A warning that the server is public comes before sending. The details of every node have a "Hikes" button: it is grey and becomes active once that node has sent us a hike (received from a message); a tap opens the map and replays it. Hike statistics (with charts of height, ascent, speed, pace and distance along the route): distance, time, average speed, ascent (when heights exist), number of points and pictures. While recording, "Range recording" runs (on by default, every 15 s): it saves the signal (RSSI and SNR) of the nodes your radio hears together with the place on the route, also when another node passes the packet on (then the signal counts for the node that passed it on). You choose how often in the recording window (5 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1 min or off). The hike window shows the number of readings and nodes, the strongest and weakest signal and a chart of the best signal along the route. The collected signal can be exported (CSV in the signal survey format, or GeoJSON with the points and the route, for further visualisation in other programs), and in the signal survey the "Import signal CSV" button adds such a file (also one from the signal survey) as a session of its own, shown by the same map and heatmap. A quiet mode for the hike (a switch) holds back notifications from all devices; the exceptions can be the favourite devices (a switch) and devices chosen by hand (a list with search); alarms and alerts always get through, and the mode ends with the recording. The source of the hike position is chosen in the recording window: best available (the default: the phone, and the device while the phone has had no position for 30 s), only the phone, only the device (the connected radio) or both at once (points from the two sources are mixed). Pictures taken with the camera also get a whole copy in a separate "MT_SW" album in the phone's pictures (on the computer in a chosen folder, by default beside the logs), so deleting a hike never deletes them. Hikes are kept on the device.
